## What stays on your device — and never reaches us

Your journey answers are stored **only in your browser's local storage, on
your own device**. This includes:

- Your selected state (e.g., "Scattered") and any note you write;
- What matters to you, what you choose, and your next action;
- Your Identity State Card.

We do **not** upload your reflections or your Identity State Card by
default, and we never send them to analytics, advertising, or third-party
AI services. If you export your card as an image, that happens entirely on
your device — where you share it is up to you.

You can delete this local information at any time with **"Clear my data"**
inside the app.

## What is stored on the server — and why

To run an invitation-only beta, we store the minimum account information
necessary:

| Information | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Email address | Invitation delivery, sign-in verification, account identity |
| Invitation record (status, expiry, redemption) | Enforce invitation-only access |
| Agreement acceptance record (agreement version, server timestamp, browser user-agent) | Evidence that you reviewed and accepted the Beta Testing Agreement |
| Feedback you choose to submit (clarity rating, comments, bug reports, follow-up permission) | Improve the product |
| Minimal usage events (journey started / journey completed — counts only, never content) | Aggregate beta metrics |

Authentication is handled by our infrastructure provider (Supabase), which
processes sign-in emails and stores account credentials securely. Data in
transit is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS. Server-side data is access-controlled:
testers can only access their own records; administrator access is limited
to the minimum necessary to run the beta (invitations, acceptance
verification, feedback, aggregate metrics).

## What we do not do

- We do not sell personal information.
- We do not use your reflections for advertising or profiling.
- We do not send your reflections to third-party AI services.
- We do not record the contents of your reflections for analytics.

## Your choices

- **Local data:** delete it anytime with "Clear my data" in the app.
- **Account / beta data:** email affinitymojo3@gmail.com to request deletion of
  your account-related beta data (invitation, acceptance, feedback, and
  event records). We will complete verified requests within 30 days.
- **Leaving the beta:** stop using the app at any time; request deletion
  as above if you want your server-side records removed.

## Changes

If this notice changes materially during the beta, we will present the new
version in the app. Continued participation after a change may require
accepting an updated Beta Testing Agreement.
